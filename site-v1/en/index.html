Process

From enquiry
to final file.

Five steps, fixed dates, a written quote before we start. No surprises on the invoice.

  1. Conversation

    Twenty minutes. You describe what should happen in the end. I say whether and how I can solve it — and what it costs.

  2. Concept

    One document: idea, visual language, schedule, shoot or generation, delivery dates. Production starts only after that.

  3. Production

    Shoot day, generative production or both. I bring camera, light and sound; models, location and styling coordinated on request.

  4. Post

    Edit, colour, sound, retouch, generative extensions. Two revision rounds are included.

  5. Delivery

    Finished files in every required format and aspect ratio, with subtitles, ready to publish.

FAQ

Before you write.

Do you film for real or generate everything?

Both — whatever the project needs. People, products, spaces and anything that has to build trust are filmed with a camera. Environments, camera moves and variants that would be unaffordable or impossible are generated. Anything generated is labelled.

How do I know what is AI?

By the label on the asset itself. Photorealistic AI-generated content is visibly marked — required by Article 50 of the EU AI Act since 2 August 2026, and it protects the credibility of your campaign.

What does a film cost?

Guide prices are above. The drivers are shooting time, crew size, number of final versions and usage rights. You receive a written fixed-price quote before anything starts.

How long does it take?

A social package is usually delivered within a week of the shoot. Brand and campaign films typically take two to four weeks from enquiry to delivery.

Who owns the finished footage?

The agreed usage rights transfer to you after full payment. Scope, channels, territory and duration are stated explicitly in the quote.

Do I need models or a location?

Not necessarily — I can arrange both. Where people are recognisable, I obtain written consent before publication.
