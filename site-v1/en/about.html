About

Ten years behind
the camera. Now
beside it too.

I'm {{{{NAME}}}}, a filmmaker and photographer based in {{{{STADT}}}}, Germany.

Over the past decade I have shot fashion productions, beauty competitions, events, interviews, documentaries, image films and product work. Camera, light, sound, edit and colour I do myself — which is why concept and result don't drift apart.

Since the first usable generations I have also worked with generative tools for image, video and audio. Not as a replacement for the camera, but as a second production layer: it allows environments, variants and movements that would otherwise be unaffordable or simply impossible.

What stayed the same: an eye for images, ease with people on set, and the ability to turn a vague idea into a concrete production plan. What changed: speed, and the number of options.
